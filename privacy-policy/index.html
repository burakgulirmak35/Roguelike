Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 21, 2026

CyberDeadZone ("we", "our", or "the game") is a free mobile game developed by Burak Gülırmak. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used when you play our game.

Information Collected

CyberDeadZone itself does not directly collect any personal information. However, our game uses Unity Ads to display advertisements. Unity Ads may collect the following data to serve ads:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (via IP address)
  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

How It Is Used

The data collected by Unity Ads is used to display relevant advertisements within the game. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties beyond what is necessary to operate the ad service.

Third-Party Services

This game uses Unity Ads. You can review Unity's privacy policy here:
https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Children's Privacy

This game is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
